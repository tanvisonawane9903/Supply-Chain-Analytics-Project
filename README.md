# Supply Chain Management Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview
The Supply Chain Management Analytics Dashboard is a data analytics project designed to analyze and monitor key supply chain operations, including orders, sales, inventory, transportation, and delivery performance.

The project uses SQL, Excel, Tableau, and Power BI to transform raw data into meaningful insights through interactive dashboards and KPI visualizations. It helps identify operational trends, monitor performance, and support data-driven business decisions.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall supply chain and order performance.
- Monitor sales and inventory trends.
- Evaluate transportation and delivery performance.
- Identify regional performance patterns.
- Develop interactive dashboards for effective business reporting.
- Track key performance indicators (KPIs).

## Tools & Technologies
- **SQL:** Data extraction, querying, filtering, and analysis.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, preparation, and analysis.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards, KPI reporting, and visualizations.
- **Tableau:** Data visualization and business performance analysis.

## Key Analysis Areas
- Order and sales analysis
- Inventory monitoring
- Transportation performance
- Delivery analysis
- Regional trends
- Operational KPI tracking

## Project Workflow
1. Data collection and understanding.
2. Data cleaning and preprocessing using Excel and SQL.
3. Data extraction, transformation, and analysis.
4. KPI identification and performance analysis.
5. Dashboard development using Power BI and Tableau.
6. Visualization of business trends and operational insights.

## Key Outcomes
- Centralized supply chain performance reporting.
- Improved visibility into orders, sales, and inventory.
- Easier monitoring of transportation and delivery operations.
- Identification of regional trends and operational performance gaps.
- Interactive reporting to support business decisions.

SQL | Data Cleaning | Data Analysis | Excel | Power BI | Tableau | KPI Reporting | Data Visualization | Business Intelligence | Supply Chain Analytics

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates the use of data analytics and business intelligence tools to convert supply chain data into actionable insights. The dashboards provide a consolidated view of operational performance and help support informed decision-making.
